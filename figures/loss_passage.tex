The solution network is evaluated at the grid points and its parameters are optimized
against the residual of the discretized problem derived in
Section~\ref{sec:approach1}. Because the equation is posed on $\Omega^{-}$ only, the
loss is assembled over the interior grid points
$I = \{\,(i,j,k) : \varphi(\mathbf{x}_{ijk}) \leq 0\,\}$; points of $\Omega^{+}$
generate no equation. We take the mean squared residual after Jacobi preconditioning,
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}(\theta)
  = \frac{1}{|I|} \sum_{(i,j,k)\, \in\, I}
    \left[
      \frac{\bigl(A\,\hat{u}_{\theta}\bigr)_{ijk} - b_{ijk}}{d_{ijk}}
    \right]^{2},
  \label{eq:loss}
\end{equation}
where $d_{ijk}$ is the diagonal entry of row $ijk$. Scaling each row by its own
diagonal equalizes the contribution of cells of differing size, so that cells cut by
$\Gamma$ -- which retain only a fraction of their nominal volume, and which carry the
Robin condition -- are weighted comparably to full interior cells.
